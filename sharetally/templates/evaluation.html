What these numbers do and do not show

  • Expectations were written next to each scenario before the engine was run, but by the same team that wrote the rules. Matching them shows the mechanism behaves as designed; it is not evidence of real-world accuracy.
  • Real records contain spelling variants, unrecorded oral partitions, missing volumes and errors that synthetic data does not reproduce. Expect more INCOMPLETE results and some false conflicts in practice.
  • Next step: blind scenarios written by a mentor from plain-language descriptions, then a pilot on anonymised, organizer-approved samples.
